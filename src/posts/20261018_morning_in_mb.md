i am searching for a god.

it surrounded us with proof
through creation;
why is it that i hesitate?

is it high-minded philosophy
that has poisoned me?
or did existentialism,
assumed incompatible, 
come naturally?

or is it
the noise of the everyday that blocks 
those thoughts that
the quiet 
of this morning 
reveals

if only 

i

listen?
